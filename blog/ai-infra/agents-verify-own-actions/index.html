What machines can check about an agent's own work

  • Execution integrity: zkVM proofs that a declared program ran on declared inputs producing declared outputs -- the rust-stark-zkvm pattern applied to agent batch steps, turning "I processed the dataset" into a verifiable receipt.
  • Structural invariants: schemas validate, types compile, tests pass, lints clean -- compilers and suites as the oldest verification gates in the building, still the highest-yield.
  • Journal integrity: hash-chained logs where each entry references its predecessor; tampering breaks the chain, per the journaling guide.
  • Policy conformance: gate verdicts recorded beside actions; a conformance checker replays sessions against policy and flags any action lacking its grant -- policy as testable specification, the APG-1 discipline.

What stays human, forever

Intent and proportionality: the agent deleted exactly the files policy allowed -- correctly, catastrophically wrong anyway. Premise truth: the task said "the report is outdated" and the agent verified every step of replacing it, none of the premise. Relevance and taste: outputs that satisfy the letter while missing the point. These are judgment calls by definition, and the mature design routes them to approval gates with context (the diff, the premise sources, the alternatives considered) rather than pretending verification covers them. The deletion-prevention guide's confirmation gates are exactly this layer: machines bound the blast radius; humans own the meaning.

The research edge: what self-verification may yet eat

Is self-verification enough for regulated workflows?

It is necessary and insufficient: regulators ask for accountability structures (owners, approvals, retention), not just correctness artifacts. Receipts satisfy the integrity column of an audit brilliantly; the authority column still needs named humans at gates and documented delegation chains. Estates that present proofs as compliance substitutes learn the distinction during the audit; estates that present proofs inside a governance story learn nothing, because the audit simply agrees -- which is the entire goal.
