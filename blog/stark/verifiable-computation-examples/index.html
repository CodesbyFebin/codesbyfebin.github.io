1-2: Proof-carrying CI and supply-chain attestations

Every release of rust-stark-zkvm attaches a STARK proof that its published benchmarks and test-vector results were produced by the pinned commit: the CI gate proves, the artifact carries, and any downstream consumer verifies in milliseconds without trusting the CI vendor. The supply-chain variant hashes build inputs (source, toolchain, flags) into the public-input vector, so a proof double as a build attestation -- reproducible builds plus verifiable execution, the two halves of "this binary came from that source" finally checked by machines rather than ceremonies. Both patterns cost seconds of CI time and have caught two drift incidents that hashes alone would have shipped.

3-4: Batch jobs and oracle transformations

Nightly batch pipelines (ETL aggregates, index builds, report generation) publish proofs of their output invariants: totals balance, row counts match sources, transformations applied in declared order. Consumers -- dashboards, auditors, downstream services -- verify instead of re-computing, which matters when the batch touches terabytes and the consumer touches kilobytes. Oracle jobs (price feeds, weather-derived indices, exchange-rate tables) prove the mapping from observed inputs to published values, converting feed operators from trusted authorities into checkable services; the federation's verification gateways expose public /verify endpoints precisely so third parties can check such claims without installing toolchains, per PRF-1.

9: Storage and hosting proofs

The federation's Merkle storage heartbeats prove continued retention of replicated blocks every cycle -- "we still host what we promised" as a checkable claim rather than an SLA paragraph, and takedown-resistance audits read the same proof chain (decentralized.hosting operations). Together the nine shapes share one grammar: private or heavy computation stays local, public receipts travel, and verification cost stays milliseconds forever. The portfolio implements seven of the nine in production today; the remaining two (verifiable ML at scale, cross-federation receipts) head the research agenda, which is this cluster's standing answer to "what's next": measured, cited, and already partially negative where the measurements said so.
