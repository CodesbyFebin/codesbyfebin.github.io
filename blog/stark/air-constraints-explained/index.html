The vanishing principle, concretely

Transition constraints: one sentence per instruction

Boundary constraints: pinning the story's ends

Transition constraints prove local legality; boundary constraints anchor global meaning: first-row pc equals zero, input registers carry the public inputs, last row has halt set and outputs in agreed columns. Without them, a perfectly legal trace of a different computation would verify -- constraints bind shape, boundaries bind identity. Keep the boundary set minimal and public: every pinned value becomes part of the public inputs the verifier checks in the clear, and bloated public inputs bloat the proof envelope and leak witness structure you may not want visible. The pinning instinct connects directly to vkey pinning at the application layer, the pair of checks the verification-failure Q&A ranks as causes one and two.

Testing constraints: negative tests are the specification

What makes an AIR unsound in practice?

Under-constrained columns: a register nobody binds on non-flag rows can drift freely between uses, and drift that never affects outputs is invisible to tests yet exploitable by a malicious prover crafting alternative witnesses. The audit is simple and brutal: for every column, name the constraint that binds every row; columns that only bind conditionally need a default relation (value carries) or removal. This column census catches more real unsoundness than any amount of positive testing.
