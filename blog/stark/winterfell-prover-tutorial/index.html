Setup and the mental split

Everything you write lives in two traits. The Trace trait fills a matrix of base-field elements row by row from your execution -- deterministic, no randomness, no clocks. The Air trait declares degrees, boundary constraints (first/last row pins), and transition constraints over a frame of current and next rows. Winterfell infers the rest: composition polynomial, DEEP queries, proof serialization format. Respecting that split is the whole tutorial; fighting it (randomness in traces, I/O in constraints) is the whole failure mode.

The three knobs, before they bite

  • Field: Security depends on the complete protocol parameters, not the base field alone.
  • Queries / folding: mostly derived from security targets; touch only with benchmarks and the cluster's honest benchmarking checklist in hand.

With those understood, your first proof is also your last surprise: everything downstream -- HTTP APIs, MCP tools, CI gates in rust-stark-zkvm -- is this hour's shape wrapped in operational discipline. The ZKVM-ISA spec shows where a full ISA's constraints land, and the cluster index routes every deeper question, from FRI internals to recursion economics.
