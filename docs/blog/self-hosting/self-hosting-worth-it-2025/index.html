The cost sheet, with the hidden line visible

Line (12 mo, typical homelab+)Cloud/SaaSSelf-hosted
Subscriptions / compute$730$0
Hardware amortized (3 yr)--$140
Power + UPS share--$110
VPS relay (CGNAT homes)--$48
Your time @ $15/h~2 h/yr configautomated: 52 h · unautomated: 156 h

At automated labor the total lands near $1,070 versus $760 in subscriptions -- wait, the cloud looks cheaper? At hobby scale it often is, financially, and pretending otherwise is how guides lose credibility. The financial win appears when subscriptions scale with usage (family photo storage, team password manager, mail for six) or when compute is steady rather than bursty; the exit-the-cloud guide's full sheets show the crossover points, typically between two and five paid seats per service.

The time truth, measured not vibes

The non-price column people quote a year later

  • Pricing immunity: vendor emails arrive as information, not decisions; budget planning stops including surprise columns.
  • Data locality: photos, journals, and health data live where your threat model says they should -- the privacy mechanics are unpacked in the cluster's privacy-benefits piece once live.
  • Egress freedom: backups and mirrors move gigabytes without a meter, which changes what you dare to keep.
  • Skill compound: the restore drill that bored you in March is the incident you solved alone in November; households report the confidence transfer to non-tech members via the one-page runbook.
